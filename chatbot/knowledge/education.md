## Why I Chose Data Science

Technology has always been my primary passion.

During the university admission process, I received admission to the Bachelor of Science in Accounting and Finance program at the Suleman Dawood School of Business (SDSB), Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS), along with a scholarship.

Although it was a valuable opportunity, I decided to pursue Data Science at PUCIT because I wanted to build a career in technology rather than business. My interests have always revolved around programming, artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation, and solving real-world problems using software.

Choosing Data Science allowed me to align my education with my long-term career goals and passion for developing intelligent systems.
