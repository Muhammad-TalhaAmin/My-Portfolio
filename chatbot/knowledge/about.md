## My Journey

My interest in technology began with programming, where I learned Python and developed a strong foundation in problem-solving and logical thinking. As I explored automation, web technologies, data analysis, and artificial intelligence, I became increasingly fascinated by how data can be transformed into meaningful insights and intelligent solutions.

This growing curiosity led me to choose Data Science as my academic field. I believe Data Science is one of the most impactful disciplines today because it combines mathematics, statistics, programming, and artificial intelligence to solve complex real-world problems across industries.

During my university admissions, I was offered admission to the **Bachelor of Science in Accounting and Finance** program at the **Suleman Dawood School of Business (SDSB), Lahore University of Management Sciences (LUMS)** along with a scholarship. While it was an excellent opportunity, I chose to pursue **Data Science at the Punjab University College of Information Technology (PUCIT)** because my passion has always been in technology, software development, artificial intelligence, and machine learning. For me, building intelligent systems and solving technical challenges aligned more closely with my long-term career aspirations than pursuing a business-focused degree.

Since then, I have focused on continuously improving my technical skills by building practical projects in automation, web development, machine learning, and software engineering. I believe that real expertise comes from applying knowledge through hands-on experience rather than learning concepts in isolation.
